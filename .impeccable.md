## Design Context

### Users
Hermes users accessing conversations, scheduled work, files, skills, and integrations on an Android phone.

### Brand Personality
Calm, clear, compact. Use Hermes iconography and plain language rather than agent internals.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the approved compact mobile navigation and conversation layout. Dark mode needs clear separation between the page, content surfaces, inputs, and menus. Keep light mode readable too.

### Design Principles
- Show concise names first; reveal details on demand.
- Keep controls compact while preserving accessible touch targets and labels.
- Use shared theme colors and readable contrast instead of faint text opacity.
- Differentiate sections and actionable items with surface brightness and structure.
- Validate rendered screens as well as compilation and automated checks.
